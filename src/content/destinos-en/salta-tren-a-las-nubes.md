## Things to do on the Train to the Clouds

The journey itself is the star: tunnels, viaducts and zigzags that let the train gain height between the landscapes of the Quebrada del Toro and the Salta Puna.

The highlight is crossing the **La Polvorilla viaduct**, where the train makes a brief stop to enjoy the view. San Antonio de los Cobres, a high-altitude town, is the stop to get to know Puna life.
