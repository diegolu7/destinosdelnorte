## Things to do in the Salinas Grandes

The salt flat's white landscape seems endless: visitors walk among salt mounds, taste the water of the wells and use the horizon for playful perspective photos.

The **Cuesta de Lipán**, on the way up, is itself an attraction with its curves and colors. Returning along the same road lets you enjoy the contrast between the salt and the mountains.
