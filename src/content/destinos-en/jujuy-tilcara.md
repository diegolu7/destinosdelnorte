## Things to do in Tilcara

The **Pucará de Tilcara** is the most important pre-Inca fortress in the region and the best-known archaeological site of the Argentine Northwest. From its ruins you get a spectacular view of the gorge.

A few kilometers away, the **Garganta del Diablo** is a canyon where the Huasamayo river runs, ideal for a short walk. The village also has a crafts market with local weavers and the Tito brothers' house museum.
