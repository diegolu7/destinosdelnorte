## Things to do in Hornocal

The star is the hill with its multicolored layers, one of the most striking landscapes in the country. From the viewpoint you can see the geological folding with its red, green and ochre tones.

The access road, along Provincial Route 73, already offers panoramic views of the gorge and the Puna that anticipate the spectacle of the viewpoint.
