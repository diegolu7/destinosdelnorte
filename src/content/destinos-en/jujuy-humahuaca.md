## Things to do in Humahuaca

Walking the cobbled streets, the square and the colonial church lets you discover the historic heart of the town. The **Independence Monument**, on a hill, gives the best panoramic view of Humahuaca and the gorge.

From the town, visits to the **Cerro del Hornocal**, with its colorful layers, and other viewpoints in the area are organized.
