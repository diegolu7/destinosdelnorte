## Things to do in Purmamarca

The classic outing is the **Paseo de los Colorados**, a circuit around the multicolored hill on foot or by vehicle. At sunset, the view from the Hill of Seven Colors viewpoint is unforgettable.

The village keeps its colonial church, dirt streets and an Andean crafts market worth a stop.
