## Things to do in Tafí del Valle

The valley invites you to explore **Route 307**, **Villa de Tafí**, the La Angostura and El Mollar dams, and the surrounding hills. Horseback rides, trekking and sport fishing are classic activities of the area.

Artisanal cheeses and regional sweets are part of the valley's gastronomic identity, together with northern dishes.
