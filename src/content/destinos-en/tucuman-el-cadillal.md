## Things to do in El Cadillal

The lake is the star: boat rides, water sports and beach areas let you enjoy the water surrounded by hills.

The viewpoints and the lakeshore complete the offer for walks, picnics and photographs of the landscape. On the way back, the mountain road offers nice views of the Tucumán hills.
