## Things to do in the Quilmes ruins

Walking the citadel lets you imagine the life of the quilme people: stone walls, the pucará above and the valley landscape as a backdrop.

From the highest area you get spectacular views of the valley and the mountain. The visit is complemented by the interpretation center and the site's history.
