## Things to do in the Quebrada de las Conchas

The route is an open-air museum: along the road appear formations like the Amphitheater, the Devil's Throat, the Frog and the Three Crosses, each with its own scale and color.

The classic stop is the Amphitheater, where the rock forms a natural amphitheater with great acoustics. The viewpoints let you stop and photograph the red and ochre canyon landscape.
