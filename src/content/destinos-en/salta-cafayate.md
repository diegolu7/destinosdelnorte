## Things to do in Cafayate

The main activity is visiting its historic wineries and learning about high-altitude wine, especially torrontés, the region's flagship variety.

The **Quebrada de las Conchas**, on the road from Salta, is a spectacle of rock formations carved by the wind: the Amphitheater, the Devil's Throat and the Three Crosses are some of the unmissable stops.
