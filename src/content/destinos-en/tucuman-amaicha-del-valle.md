## Things to do in Amaicha del Valle

Visiting the **Pachamama Museum**, which gathers sculptures and objects telling the region's history and beliefs, is a must. The town also celebrates the Fiesta de la Pachamama in February, the largest in the area.

A few kilometers away lie the **Quilmes ruins**, the country's largest archaeological site, and the landscape of the Amaicha gorge with its cardón cacti and historic vineyards.
