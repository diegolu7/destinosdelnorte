## Things to do in Cachi

Walking the historic center is the best way to know the village: the square, the church and its dirt streets with colonial houses invite you to wander among cardón cacti and mountains.

Nearby you can explore the valleys, high-altitude wineries and the landscapes of Los Cardones National Park, with its cardón forest and the famous Cuesta del Obispo.
