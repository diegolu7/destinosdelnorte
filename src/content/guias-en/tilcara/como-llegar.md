## From San Salvador de Jujuy

Tilcara is about **85 km from San Salvador de Jujuy** on National Route 9. By car it takes about **2 hours** through the Humahuaca Gorge.

## By tourist train

A different option is arriving by **train from the Jujuy capital**, a scenic ride through the gorge.

## By bus

Regular buses connect Jujuy with Tilcara and continue to Humahuaca, making it a practical base to explore the gorge.
