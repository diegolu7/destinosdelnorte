## April to November

The months of **April to November** offer the best weather to explore Tilcara: clear, pleasant days ideal for the Pucará and the Garganta del Diablo.

## August and the Pachamama

In **August** the Pachamama festivities take place, a very special cultural time to learn about the gorge's traditions. There are more visitors.

## Summer and winter

In **summer** rains are more likely. In **winter** days are nice but nights are cold; Tilcara's altitude (over 2,400 m) is felt.
