## April to November

The months of **April to November** offer clear skies that highlight the salt's white and the reflections on the horizon.

## Summer

In **summer** rains are more likely, which can leave the salt flat with water and complicate some accesses. Reflections over the water can still be spectacular.

## Tip

The **altitude** (over 3,000 m) is felt: go hydrated and with sunscreen and sunglasses, as the sun's reflection over the salt is intense.
