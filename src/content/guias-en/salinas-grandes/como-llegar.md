## From Purmamarca through the Cuesta de Lipán

The most common access is from **Purmamarca** through the **Cuesta de Lipán**, about **60 km** away. The road climbs over 4,000 meters and offers spectacular views before reaching the salt flat.

## By tour

Many **tours** leave from San Salvador de Jujuy or Purmamarca and combine the Salinas Grandes with the Hill of Seven Colors.

## Recommendation

The road is high-altitude with curves. Go with time, stay hydrated and check the road before leaving.
