## Spring and autumn

These are the **best seasons** to visit Cafayate, with mild weather and sunny days ideal for wineries and the Quebrada de las Conchas.

## Harvest in March

Every March the **harvest festival** takes place, the most special time of the year to experience the winemaking tradition. It is also the busiest time, so book ahead.

## Summer and winter

In **summer** there is more rain and high daytime temperatures. In **winter** days are pleasant but nights get cold, so bring warm clothes.
