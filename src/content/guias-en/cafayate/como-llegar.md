## By car from Salta

From the city of Salta it is about **190 km on National Route 68**, crossing the Calchaquí Valleys. The drive, with stops in the Quebrada de las Conchas, takes about **3 hours**.

## By tour

It is the most chosen option for those not driving. Most tours leave Salta in the morning, include stops at the Amphitheater and the Devil's Throat, and return the same day.

## By bus

There are regular long-distance services connecting Salta with Cafayate. Check schedules and frequency, which can vary by season.
