## Spring and autumn

**Spring and autumn** are the best seasons, with pleasant weather to explore the town, the Pachamama Museum and the Quilmes ruins.

## Summer

In **summer** it is very hot during the day and rains can complicate the roads. Bring water and sunscreen.

## Winter

In **winter** days are pleasant and **nights are cold**. The altitude and dry climate of the Calchaquí Valleys are felt, so dress warmly.
