## From Tafí del Valle

It is reached from **Tafí del Valle** on **Route 307**, a scenic stretch through the Tucumán Calchaquí Valleys.

## From Cafayate

It can also be combined from **Cafayate** on **Route 40**, heading south. It is a common stop on the Calchaquí Valleys route.

## Recommendation

Amaicha del Valle is a practical base to visit the **Quilmes ruins**, a few kilometers away on Route 40, and to continue to Tafí del Valle or Cafayate.
