## Spring and autumn

**Spring and autumn** are ideal to explore the ruins: pleasant temperatures for walking the citadel and the viewpoints.

## Summer

In **summer** it is very hot during the day and there is little shade on the site. Bring water, sunscreen and a hat.

## Winter

In **winter** nights are cold but days are usually sunny. The altitude and dry climate are felt; bring warm clothes for the night.
