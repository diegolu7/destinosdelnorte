## From Amaicha del Valle

The ruins are about **5 km from Amaicha del Valle**, on **National Route 40**, on the border with Catamarca.

## From Tafí del Valle or Cafayate

They are visited as part of the Calchaquí Valleys route, by **car or tour** from Amaicha, Tafí del Valle or Cafayate.

## Recommendation

The site has an **interpretation center** and marked trails. Bring water and sunscreen; the walk is outdoors with little shade.
