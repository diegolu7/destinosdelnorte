## From San Miguel de Tucumán

From the Tucumán capital it is about **108 km on Route 307**. The road climbs the **Cuesta del Clavillo** and runs beside La Angostura dam until it reaches the valley.

## By tour or bus

It is common to do it by **tour** from San Miguel de Tucumán, with stops at the cuesta and the dam. There are also **buses** connecting the capital with the valley.

## Driving tip

Route 307 is scenic but with curves and gradients; drive carefully and stop to enjoy the views.
