## All year round

Tafí del Valle is a destination for **all year round**. In **summer** it is a mild-weather refuge from the heat of the Tucumán plains; in **winter** the valley is covered in mist and cold with imposing landscapes.

## Spring and autumn

**Spring and autumn** offer the ideal balance: pleasant days for walks, trekking and outings by the lake, without summer heat or intense winter cold.

## Tip

**Nights are cold** all year in the valley. Bring warm clothes even in hot season.
