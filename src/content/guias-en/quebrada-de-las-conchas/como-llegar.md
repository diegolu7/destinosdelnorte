## On National Route 68

The gorge runs along **National Route 68**, connecting Salta city with **Cafayate**. By car you can stop at the viewpoints and formations (Amphitheater, Devil's Throat, Three Crosses).

## By tour

It is also visited as part of the **tours to Cafayate** leaving from Salta, with stops at the best-known points of the canyon.

## Recommendation

Allow time to enjoy the drive without rushing. Some sections have no shade, so bring water and sunscreen.
