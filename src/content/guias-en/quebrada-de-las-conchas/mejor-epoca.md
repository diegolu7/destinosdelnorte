## Spring and autumn

**Spring and autumn** are ideal, with mild weather and clear skies that highlight the colors of the rock formations.

## Summer

In **summer** rains can affect some sections of the road. Check the condition of Route 68 if traveling then.

## Recommendation

The best light for photographing the canyon is usually in the **morning and at sunset**. Avoid the hottest hours in summer.
