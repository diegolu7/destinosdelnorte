## From Humahuaca

Access to the **Hornocal** (Hill of the Fourteen Colors) is on **Provincial Route 73**, about **25 km from Humahuaca**, plus a few more kilometers of mountain road to the **viewpoint**.

## By vehicle

It is reached in your **own vehicle**, with **4x4 recommended** on some stretches, or by **tour** from Humahuaca. The high-altitude road is slow and winding.

## Recommendation

Check the **road condition** before going, as it can be tricky after rain. The viewpoint is over 4,000 m: go warm and take it slow.
