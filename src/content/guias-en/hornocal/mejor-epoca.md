## April to November

The best season runs from **April to November**, with clear skies that bring out the hill's color layers.

## Sunset

In the **afternoon**, light usually enhances the Hornocal's colors. Many travelers recommend arriving near sunset to see the hill at its best.

## Summer

In **summer** rains can complicate the access road. Check the condition of Route 73 before going.
