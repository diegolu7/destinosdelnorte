## Spring and autumn

**Spring and autumn** offer the best weather to visit Cachi: mild, sunny days ideal for the village and the Calchaquí Valleys.

## Summer

In **summer** there is more rain, which can complicate the Cuesta del Obispo. If traveling then, check the road.

## Winter

In **winter** days are pleasant but **nights are cold**, as Cachi is over 2,200 meters high. Bring warm clothes.
