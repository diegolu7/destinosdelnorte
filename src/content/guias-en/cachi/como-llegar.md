## From Salta

From the city of Salta it is about **160 km on Provincial Route 33**, climbing the **Cuesta del Obispo** and crossing **Los Cardones National Park**. The drive is scenic and takes about **3 hours** with stops.

## By tour

It is common to take a **tour from Salta**, with stops at the Cuesta del Obispo and the cardón landscape. It can also be combined with Cafayate through the Calchaquí Valleys.

## Driving tip

Route 33 has curves and high-altitude gradients. Drive carefully and check the road condition, especially in the rainy season.
