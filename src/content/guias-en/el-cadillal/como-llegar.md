## From San Miguel de Tucumán

El Cadillal is about **20 km from San Miguel de Tucumán** on **Provincial Route 340**. By car it takes about **half an hour**.

## By tour or regular service

It can be done by **tour** from the Tucumán capital, or on regular services toward the dam area. It is a classic weekend escape.

## Recommendation

The lake and its viewpoints are best enjoyed with time. Check the **water activities** and their requirements on site.
