## All year round

El Cadillal is a **year-round** destination. In **summer** the beaches and lake fill with visitors; in **autumn and spring** the weather is ideal for walks and viewpoints.

## Weekend

It is a classic **long-weekend** escape, when the lake receives more visitors. If you seek quiet, prefer weekdays.

## Tip

Bring **sunscreen**; by the lake the sun feels strong. Check water activities and their hours.
