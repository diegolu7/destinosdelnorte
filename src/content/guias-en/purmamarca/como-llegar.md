## From San Salvador de Jujuy

Purmamarca is about **70 km from San Salvador de Jujuy** on National Route 9. By car it takes about **1.5 hours**.

## From Tilcara and Humahuaca

On the same National Route 9, Tilcara is about **65 km** away and Humahuaca further north. Many travelers plan the Humahuaca Gorge route including Purmamarca as a stop.

## By bus or tour

There are buses from the Jujuy capital and tours combining Purmamarca with the Hill of Seven Colors and the Salinas Grandes.
