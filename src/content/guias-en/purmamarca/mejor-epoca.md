## Spring and autumn

The best season runs from **April to November**, with clear days that bring out the colors of the Hill of Seven Colors. Early morning or sunset, the hill's light is strongest.

## Summer

In **summer** rains are more likely, which can affect the roads and the walk around the hill. Still, the gorge's vegetation looks greener.

## Winter

In **winter** days are sunny and pleasant, but **nights are cold**. Bring warm clothes for the night and comfortable clothes for the day.
