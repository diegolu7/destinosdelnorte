## Departure from Salta

The **Train to the Clouds** leaves from the station in **Salta city**. The route crosses the Quebrada del Toro, the Puna and ends at the **La Polvorilla viaduct**, over 4,200 meters high.

## Dates and bookings

Services run on **dates and times that vary by season**. Check with the **official operator** and book well in advance, especially in high season.

## The journey

It is a same-day round trip. It includes a stop in **San Antonio de los Cobres** and the highlight at the viaduct, with Puna landscapes.
