## April to November

The months of **April to November** usually offer clearer skies, ideal for the high-altitude landscapes. Always check the **departure dates** with the official operator.

## Any season

The train runs **all year**, but high-altitude conditions (over 4,000 m) are felt: go well hydrated and with warm clothes even on sunny days.

## Tip

Book with **plenty of notice**, as seats sell out in high season and on weekends.
