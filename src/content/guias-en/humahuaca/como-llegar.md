## From San Salvador de Jujuy

Humahuaca is about **125 km from San Salvador de Jujuy** on **National Route 9**, crossing the Humahuaca Gorge. By car it takes about **2.5 hours**.

## By bus or tour

**Buses** connect Jujuy with Humahuaca (passing through Tilcara). It is also reached by **tour** from the Jujuy capital, with stops in the gorge towns.

## From Tilcara

From Tilcara, Humahuaca is about **20 km** north, a short, scenic stretch on the same route.
