## April to November

The best season runs from **April to November**, with clear days that highlight the gorge's colors and the views from the viewpoints.

## August and the Pachamama

In **August** the **Pachamama** festivities take place, a very special cultural moment in Humahuaca and the whole gorge.

## Winter

In **winter** days are sunny but **nights are cold**: Humahuaca is over 3,000 meters, so bring warm clothes.
