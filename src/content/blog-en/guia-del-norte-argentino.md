## How to get there

The most comfortable way is to **fly**: Salta, San Salvador de Jujuy and San Miguel de Tucumán have airports with regular flights from Buenos Aires. By car, **National Route 9** links Salta and Jujuy through the Humahuaca Gorge, and **Route 68** connects Salta with Cafayate.

## Best time

**Spring and autumn** (April to November) offer the best overall weather. In **summer** there is more rain and heat; in **winter** days are pleasant but nights are cold, especially in the mountains.

## Getting around

Many destinations are reached by **bus** or **day tours** from the provincial capitals. Having your **own car** (ideally with decent ground clearance) gives you the freedom to stop at viewpoints and combine nearby places, like the Calchaquí Valleys or the Humahuaca Gorge.

## The altitude

Much of the North sits above 2,000 meters (and places like the Salinas Grandes exceed 3,000). Take it easy the first day, stay hydrated and check with your doctor if you have any health conditions.

## What to pack

Clothing in **layers**, warm clothes for the nights, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and water. The rule is to adapt to the intense high-altitude sun and the cold nights.
