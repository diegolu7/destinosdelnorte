Salta offers several attractions that **charge no entry fee** and only ask you to go and enjoy them. Here are some must-sees.

> **Important:** the attractions themselves are **free to visit**. What you do pay, when you go to a farther-away town, is the **transport or tour** to get there (not the ticket). In contrast, the hills and viewpoints **inside the city** are done on your own, without a tour.

## Cerro de la Virgen

A classic walk **from the city of Salta**, reached on foot, with **panoramic views** of the valley and San Bernardo Hill. Since it is in the city, you need no tour or transport: it is 100% free.

## Viewpoints of the Calchaquí Valleys

Much of the North's landscape is enjoyed **stopping at the viewpoints** along the roads (Quebrada de las Conchas, Cuesta del Obispo, Cuesta de Lipán). They have no cost and offer some of the best views.

## The Quebrada de las Conchas

Driving the **[Quebrada de las Conchas](/en/salta/quebrada-de-las-conchas/)** by car, stopping at formations like the Amphitheater and the Devil's Throat, is a **free** plan enjoyed along Route 68.

## Cerro Elefante

In the **[Cachi](/en/salta/cachi/)** area, the Cerro Elefante is a formation shaped like an elephant that can be seen from the Calchaquí Valleys. The view is **free**; if you don't go in your own car, what you pay is the **transport or tour** to reach Cachi.
