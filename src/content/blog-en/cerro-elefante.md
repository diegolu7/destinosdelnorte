## What it is

**Cerro Elefante** is a rock formation whose silhouette resembles an elephant, located in the **Calchaquí Valleys**, near **[Cachi](/en/salta/cachi/)**.

## Why visit it

The view of **Cerro Elefante** is a **free attraction**: the formation is seen from the viewpoint, with no entry fee. What you do pay, if you don't go in your own car, is the **transport or tour** to reach the **[Cachi](/en/salta/cachi/)** area.

## How to include it

It is usually visited on the stretch between Salta, Cachi and Cafayate. As it is a stop along the way, it needs no significant extra time and combines with the landscapes of the area.
