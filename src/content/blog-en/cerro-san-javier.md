## What it is

**Cerro San Javier** is a rise near **San Miguel de Tucumán**, surrounded by nature, trails and viewpoints. It is a **free** destination to walk, breathe fresh air and take in the views of the city and the plains.

## Why visit it

It is a step away from the Tucumán capital, so it is reached **in the day without needing a tour**. The visit itself is **free**; you only pay transport if you don't have your own car to get there.

## How to include it

It can be combined with Tucumán's mountain destinations, such as **[Tafí del Valle](/en/tucuman/tafi-del-valle/)** or **[El Cadillal](/en/tucuman/el-cadillal/)**, or enjoyed as a quick outing from the city.
