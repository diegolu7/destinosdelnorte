## What it is

**Cerro de la Virgen** is a hill on the outskirts of the city of **Salta**, from where you get **panoramic views** of the Lerma valley and San Bernardo Hill.

## How to get there

Access is **on foot, from the city**: no tour or transport needed, so it is 100% **free** and easy to do in a day while visiting Salta.

## Tip

Bring water and go with time, preferably in the morning or at sunset to enjoy the views better. Combine the visit with a stroll through Salta's historic center.
