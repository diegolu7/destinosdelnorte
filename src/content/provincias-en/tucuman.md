## Places to visit in Tucumán

San Miguel de Tucumán preserves the House of Independence and a center with colonial and neoclassical architecture. At the foot of the mountains, Tafí del Valle and Amaicha del Valle offer mountains, fresh air and ancestral traditions.

The Quilmes ruins, the country's most important archaeological site, lie on the border with Catamarca and are a must for those seeking history. The El Cadillal dam and San Pedro de Colalao complete the natural offer.

## Things to do in Tucumán

- **San Miguel de Tucumán**: the House of Independence and the historic center.
- **Tafí del Valle**: hills, dams, artisanal cheeses and horseback rides.
- **Amaicha del Valle**: the Pachamama, its festivals and the Pachamama Museum.
- **Quilmes ruins**: the largest pre-Hispanic citadel in Argentina.
- **El Cadillal**: a great dam surrounded by hills a few kilometers from the capital.

## Best time to visit Tucumán

Spring and autumn offer the best weather to explore the valleys. In summer temperatures rise and there is more rain; winter is dry with cool nights in the mountains.

## How many days to stay

With 2 days you can combine the city with Tafí del Valle. To add Amaicha del Valle, Quilmes and El Cadillal at a relaxed pace, allow 4 days.
