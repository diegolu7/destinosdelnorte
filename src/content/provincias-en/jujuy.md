## Places to visit in Jujuy

National Route 9 runs through the Humahuaca Gorge connecting its most iconic towns: Purmamarca with the Hill of Seven Colors, Tilcara with its pucará and market, and Humahuaca with its church and Independence monument.

To the west lie the Salinas Grandes, one of South America's largest salt flats, while the Puna stretches into high-altitude landscapes reaching 4,000 meters above sea level.

## Things to do in Jujuy

- **Purmamarca and the Hill of Seven Colors**: one of Argentina's most photographed landscapes.
- **Tilcara**: the Pucará, the Garganta del Diablo and its crafts market.
- **Humahuaca**: the gorge's highest town and its panoramic views.
- **Salinas Grandes**: explore the salt flat and the Cuesta de Lipán on the way to the Puna.
- **Towns with tradition**: Maimará, Uquía and the colorful hills of the gorge.

## Best time to visit Jujuy

The best season runs from April to November, with sunny days and dry weather. In summer, rains can make some roads in the gorge more difficult.

## How many days to stay

With 2 or 3 days you can explore the Humahuaca Gorge comfortably from San Salvador de Jujuy. To add the Salinas Grandes and the Puna, allow at least 4 days.
