## Places to visit in Salta

The capital, known as "Salta la Linda", invites you to explore its historic center, churches and the famous cable car up to San Bernardo hill. Nearby, the Lerma and Escoipe valleys offer scenic mountain routes.

Cafayate is the province's star destination for wine tourism, with historic wineries and the spectacular Quebrada de las Conchas. Further north through the Calchaquí Valleys, Cachi combines colonial heritage with high-altitude landscapes.

## Things to do in Salta

- **Explore the city**: 9 de Julio square, the Cabildo, the Cathedral and the cable car to San Bernardo hill.
- **Wine route in Cafayate**: wineries, high-altitude vineyards and tastings.
- **Quebrada de las Conchas**: colorful rock formations between Salta and Cafayate.
- **Train to the Clouds**: one of the highest railway journeys in the world.
- **Calchaquí Valleys villages**: Cachi, Molinos, Seclantás and their traditions.

## Best time to visit Salta

Spring and autumn offer the best weather, with mild temperatures and sunny days. It rains more in summer, and in winter nights are cool but days are usually pleasant.

## How many days to stay

With 3 days you can visit the city and a day trip to Cafayate. To also explore the Calchaquí Valleys up to Cachi, plan at least 5 days.
