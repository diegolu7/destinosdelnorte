## Day 1 · Arrival in Salta

Arrive in **Salta** and walk the historic center: the 9 de Julio square, the Cabildo and the Cathedral. At sunset, San Bernardo Hill or the cable car offer great views.

## Day 2 · Salta to Cafayate

Drive along **National Route 68** through the **Quebrada de las Conchas** (Amphitheater, Devil's Throat). End in **[Cafayate](/en/salta/cafayate/)** and visit its wineries.

## Day 3 · Cafayate and the Calchaquí Valleys

Enjoy the morning among Cafayate's vineyards and wineries. In the afternoon you can continue toward **Cachi** through the Calchaquí Valleys, passing the cardón landscapes.

## Day 4 · Cachi to Jujuy

Tour **Cachi** and its colonial church. Then head on to **San Salvador de Jujuy**, getting ready to enter the Humahuaca Gorge.

## Day 5 · Purmamarca and the Salinas Grandes

Visit **[Purmamarca](/en/jujuy/purmamarca/)** and its **Hill of Seven Colors**, and climb the **Cuesta de Lipán** to the **Salinas Grandes**.

## Day 6 · Tilcara and Humahuaca

Tour **[Tilcara](/en/jujuy/tilcara/)** with its Pucará and the Garganta del Diablo, and continue to **Humahuaca** to see the Independence Monument.

## Day 7 · Return

Use the morning for the **Hornocal** or to stroll the gorge, and head back to your starting point.
