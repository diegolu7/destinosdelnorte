## Day 1 · Salta

Walk through the historic center of **Salta** and ride up **San Bernardo Hill** for views of the city.

## Day 2 · Quebrada de las Conchas and Cafayate

Drive along **National Route 68** toward **Cafayate**, stopping in the **Quebrada de las Conchas**. Visit a winery and spend the night in the valley.

## Day 3 · Purmamarca

Head back toward **Jujuy** and enter the gorge through **[Purmamarca](/en/jujuy/purmamarca/)**, with its **Hill of Seven Colors** and the Colorados walk.

## Day 4 · Tilcara and Humahuaca

Explore **[Tilcara](/en/jujuy/tilcara/)** (Pucará and Garganta del Diablo) and continue to **Humahuaca** for the viewpoints and the Independence Monument.

## Day 5 · Salinas Grandes and return

Climb the **Cuesta de Lipán** to the **Salinas Grandes** and head back, closing the loop at a relaxed pace.
