## Day 1 · Salta to Cachi

Leave **Salta** along **Provincial Route 33**, crossing the Cuesta del Obispo and Los Cardones National Park until **[Cachi](/en/salta/cachi/)**.

## Day 2 · Cachi to Cafayate

Continue through the Calchaquí Valleys to **[Cafayate](/en/salta/cafayate/)**. In the afternoon, wineries and high-altitude wines.

## Day 3 · Cafayate to Amaicha del Valle

Head south on **Route 40** to **[Amaicha del Valle](/en/tucuman/amaicha-del-valle/)**. Visit the Pachamama Museum.

## Day 4 · Quilmes ruins

Spend the morning at the **[Quilmes ruins](/en/tucuman/ruinas-de-quilmes/)** and its interpretation center. In the afternoon, relax in the valley.

## Day 5 · Tafí del Valle

Travel to **[Tafí del Valle](/en/tucuman/tafi-del-valle/)** on Route 307. Enjoy the lake, the walks and the artisanal cheeses.

## Day 6 · Return

Enjoy the morning in Tafí del Valle and head back toward San Miguel de Tucumán or Salta.
