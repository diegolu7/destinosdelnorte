## Day 1 · San Miguel de Tucumán to Tafí del Valle

Leave the capital for **[Tafí del Valle](/en/tucuman/tafi-del-valle/)** on Route 307 and the Cuesta del Clavillo. In the afternoon, the lake and the town.

## Day 2 · Amaicha del Valle

Travel to **[Amaicha del Valle](/en/tucuman/amaicha-del-valle/)**. Visit the Pachamama Museum and the landscapes of the gorge.

## Day 3 · Quilmes ruins

Tour the **[Quilmes ruins](/en/tucuman/ruinas-de-quilmes/)** in the morning and return to Tafí del Valle for the afternoon and evening.

## Day 4 · El Cadillal and return

On the way back to the city, stop at **[El Cadillal](/en/tucuman/el-cadillal/)** to enjoy the lake and its viewpoints before closing the trip.
