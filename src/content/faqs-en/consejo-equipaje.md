---
pregunta: "What should I pack for the Argentine North?"
categoria: "Tips"
---

The rule is **dressing in layers**. Bring comfortable clothes for the day, a **jacket or warm layer** for the nights and the mountains, **sunscreen and sunglasses** (the high-altitude sun is intense), a hat, water and comfortable walking shoes. In summer add fresh clothes and something for the rain; in winter, real warm clothes for the cold nights.
