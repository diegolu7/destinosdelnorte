---
pregunta: "How do I get to Salta, Jujuy or Tucumán?"
categoria: "How to get there"
---

The most comfortable way is to **fly**: Salta, Jujuy (San Salvador de Jujuy) and Tucumán (San Miguel de Tucumán) have airports with regular flights from Buenos Aires. By car, National Route 9 links Salta and Jujuy through the Humahuaca Gorge, and National Route 68 connects Salta with Cafayate. You can see details per destination in guides like [how to get to Cafayate](/en/salta/cafayate/como-llegar/).
