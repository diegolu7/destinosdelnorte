The Argentine North offers very varied experiences: **wineries and high-altitude wines** (Cafayate), **hiking and nature** (Tafí del Valle, the Humahuaca Gorge), **culture and history** (Purmamarca, Tilcara, Quilmes ruins) and **adventure** in high-altitude landscapes.
