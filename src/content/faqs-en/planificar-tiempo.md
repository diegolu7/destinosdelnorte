---
pregunta: "How much time do I need to travel the Argentine North?"
categoria: "Planning your trip"
---

Ideally, allow at least **one week** to combine the three provinces at a relaxed pace. With 5 days you can build a good base in Salta and Jujuy (Cafayate, Purmamarca and Tilcara). To add Tucumán (Tafí del Valle and Amaicha del Valle), it is best to plan 8 to 10 days. If your time is short, pick a single province and explore it in depth.
