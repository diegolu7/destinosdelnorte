---
pregunta: "How do I prepare for the altitude?"
categoria: "Tips"
---

Many destinations in the North are above **2,000 and up to 4,000 meters** (Salinas Grandes, Hornocal, Train to the Clouds). To adapt: **take it easy on the first day**, stay well hydrated, avoid intense effort on arrival and check with your doctor if you have any health conditions. Altitude sickness is mild in most cases and improves with rest and hydration.
