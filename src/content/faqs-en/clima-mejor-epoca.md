---
pregunta: "What is the best time to visit the Argentine North?"
categoria: "Climate and best time"
---

**Spring and autumn** (April to November) offer the best overall weather: mild, dry and sunny days. In **summer** (December to March) there is more rain and heat, but the vegetation is greener. In **winter** days are pleasant but nights are cold, especially in the mountains. It is a region that can be visited all year, adapting your packing.
