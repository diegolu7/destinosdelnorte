---
pregunta: "What is the weather like in the Argentine North?"
categoria: "Climate and best time"
---

The weather varies a lot with **altitude**. In the cities (Salta, San Miguel de Tucumán) it is hot in summer and mild the rest of the year. In the mountains (Cachi, Tafí del Valle, the Humahuaca Gorge) days are sunny and **nights are cold**, even in summer. In the Puna (Salinas Grandes, Hornocal) the sun is intense and temperatures are low. The key is **dressing in layers**.
