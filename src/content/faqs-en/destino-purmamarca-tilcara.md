---
pregunta: "What is the difference between Purmamarca and Tilcara?"
categoria: "Destinations"
---

Both are in the **Humahuaca Gorge**, but they are different. **[Purmamarca](/en/jujuy/purmamarca/)** is a small village famous for the **Hill of Seven Colors**, ideal for a short visit and photos. **[Tilcara](/en/jujuy/tilcara/)** is bigger, with the **Pucará** (pre-Inca fortress), the Garganta del Diablo and a handicraft market. Many travelers visit both in the same day.
