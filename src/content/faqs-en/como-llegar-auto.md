---
pregunta: "Do I need a car to travel the Argentine North?"
categoria: "How to get there"
---

It is not essential, but it **helps a lot**. Many destinations are reached by bus or organized tours from the provincial capitals. However, having your own car (ideally with decent ground clearance for mountain roads) gives you the freedom to stop at viewpoints and combine nearby places. On destinations like the [Hornocal](/en/jujuy/hornocal/) or the [Salinas Grandes](/en/jujuy/salinas-grandes/), a vehicle suited for the high-altitude roads is recommended.
