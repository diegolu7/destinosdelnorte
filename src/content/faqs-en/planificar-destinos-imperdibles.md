---
pregunta: "Which destinations in the Argentine North can I not miss?"
categoria: "Planning your trip"
---

The must-sees vary by how much time you have, but the favorites are **[Cafayate](/en/salta/cafayate/)** for its wineries and the Quebrada de las Conchas, **[Purmamarca](/en/jujuy/purmamarca/)** for the Hill of Seven Colors, **[Tilcara](/en/jujuy/tilcara/)** for its pucará, and **[Tafí del Valle](/en/tucuman/tafi-del-valle/)** for its mountains. If you look for unique landscapes, add the **[Salinas Grandes](/en/jujuy/salinas-grandes/)** and the **[Hornocal](/en/jujuy/hornocal/)**.
