---
pregunta: "What to do in Cafayate?"
categoria: "Destinations"
---

In **[Cafayate](/en/salta/cafayate/)** the main activity is **visiting wineries** and tasting high-altitude wines, especially torrontés. It is also essential to visit the **Quebrada de las Conchas**, with formations like the Amphitheater and the Devil's Throat, on the road from Salta. It is an ideal destination to combine wine, landscape and peace.
